You may use the RiftDeck name:

* To truthfully refer to the official RiftDeck project

* "Based on RiftDeck"

If you distribute a modified version or fork of RiftDeck, you must not represent it as an official RiftDeck release.

Unless you have received explicit permission, modified distributions should:

* Use a different application name
* Use a different application icon and logo
* Clearly state that the project is derived from RiftDeck

You may describe your project as:

> Based on the RiftDeck open-source project.

You may not present the modified project itself as **RiftDeck**.
